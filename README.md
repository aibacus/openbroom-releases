## Why people use OpenBroom

<table>
<tr>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

### 🔍 Every item explained
Each thing OpenBroom finds says what it is, where it lives and what happens if you remove it. No mystery categories.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

### ♻️ Trash first, with undo
Cleaning moves files to the Trash, never straight to deletion. Changed your mind? Put the last cleanup back.

<td width="33%" valign="top">

### 🔒 Stays on your Mac
No account and no sign-in. Scans run on your Mac, and your files and results are not sent anywhere.

</td>

<tr>
<td valign="top">

Only items labelled **Safe** are preselected. Your own photos, videos and documents are never selected for you.

</td>
<td valign="top">

System folders and other locations macOS depends on are refused in code, so they can't be removed by mistake.

</td>
<td valign="top">

### ⚡ Fast and native
Written in Swift for macOS. One Sweep looks through junk, developer files, apps, startup items and more.

## How it works

| | | |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| **1. Scan** | **2. Understand** | **3. Clean** |
| Press Scan. Scanning only looks; it never deletes anything. | Every item has a label (**Safe**, **Check first** or **Your files**) and a plain explanation. | Review the selection and press Clean. Files go to the Trash, and the last cleanup can be undone. |

<br>

## Get OpenBroom

**Requirements:** macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later).

1. Download the latest version from the **[OpenBroom website](https://openbroom.rokib.dev)**.
2. Open the downloaded file and drag **OpenBroom** to your **Applications** folder.
3. Open OpenBroom and press **Scan**.

## Questions

<details>
<summary><b>Is it safe?</b></summary>
<br>
OpenBroom is built to be careful. Scanning never deletes anything. Cleaning moves files to the Trash, protected system locations are refused, projects with uncommitted changes are skipped, and only items labelled Safe are preselected.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What exactly does it remove?</b></summary>
<br>
Only what you select and confirm. Typical items are app caches, logs, old installers, unused language files, developer build output, package caches and app leftovers.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Why might it ask for Full Disk Access?</b></summary>
<br>
macOS protects some locations, such as Mail and Safari data. OpenBroom asks only when a feature needs one of them, and everything else works without it. You can say no.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Will it make my Mac faster?</b></summary>
<br>
OpenBroom frees disk space and shows what starts with your Mac and which apps use the most memory. It does not promise a faster Mac and does not offer "memory cleaning".
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does it send my data anywhere?</b></summary>
<br>
No. Scans and results stay on your Mac, and there is no account. The only network request is the update check, and it runs only when you ask for it.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What does it cost?</b></summary>
<br>
OpenBroom is free to download.
</details>
